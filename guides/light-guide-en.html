How to Import XMP Presets in Lightroom Classic

English 日本語 Deutsch Français Italiano

Lightroom Classic development presets are not interchangeable with video LUTs.

Step-by-step

  1. Check your download first. For Lightroom Classic, look for compatible .xmp presets or a ZIP containing the preset files. Do not confuse XMP Develop presets with video .cube LUTs.
  2. Open Lightroom Classic and switch to Develop. Find the Presets panel.
  3. Click the + button in Presets and choose Import Presets.
  4. Select the compatible preset file(s) or a supported .zip archive and confirm Import. Adobe says imported presets are added under User Presets.
  5. Open a representative image, preview the preset and apply it. Review exposure, skin tones and white balance. If available, adjust the Preset Amount slider.

Troubleshooting

If the preset does not appear, inspect its format, your Lightroom version and whether the preset only supports particular raw files or camera profiles. Adobe documents partially compatible presets.

Source: Official Adobe Lightroom Classic documentation. Interface labels can change between versions.
