Lightroom Mobile Presets: How to Import DNG and XMP Correctly

English 日本語 Deutsch Français Italiano

Lightroom mobile import options differ between versions and platforms. DNG-based sample presets and XMP settings are not interchangeable with video LUT files.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Inspect the pack contents before import. A photo preset might come as XMP files, DNG examples or a supported preset pack; a .cube video LUT is not the same thing.
  2. Update Adobe Lightroom mobile, open a photo and find the Presets panel. Look for the menu to import presets or manage preset groups in your installed version.
  3. If the app supports XMP/ZIP import, choose the compatible files. If you have only DNG example photos, import a DNG image and use its settings as a starting point for creating your own preset, when your version supports it.
  4. Apply the new preset to a test photo. Check exposure, skin tones and whether the source is RAW or JPEG.
  5. If a preset appears missing or partially compatible, confirm app version, cloud sync status and whether the pack requires a camera profile that you do not have.

Common problems

A DNG is a digital negative image, not automatically a ready-made Lightroom preset file. Imported images and preset files may follow different workflows.

Before buying a LUT or preset

Choose a pack specifically labeled for the mobile version and correct import format; desktop Lightroom Classic XMP instructions may not apply to all mobile releases.
