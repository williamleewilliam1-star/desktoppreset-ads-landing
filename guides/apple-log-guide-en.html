Apple Log LUTs: A Reliable iPhone Color Grading Workflow

English 日本語 Deutsch Français Italiano

Apple Log is a recording curve, not a finished cinematic look. Identify your recording settings before adding a LUT.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Check whether your iPhone video was actually recorded in Apple Log. Standard iPhone footage and Apple Log should not be treated identically.
  2. Import your original video into an editor that supports the recorded format and a predictable color-management workflow.
  3. Apply a correct technical transform for the relevant Apple Log version and target color space when your editor has not already managed the conversion. Avoid converting twice.
  4. After normalization, apply a creative LUT that expects that output color space, then adjust exposure, strength and skin tones.
  5. Export a short reference clip in the intended delivery color space, compare it on your target device, and retain the untreated original.

Common problems

Why does the video look gray? Log footage intentionally uses a flatter encoding. Why is it too contrasty? A conversion may have been applied twice, or the creative LUT expects a different input.

Before buying a LUT or preset

Do not assume that a pack labeled cinematic automatically contains the technical Apple Log transform. Confirm the recording format, color space, .cube compatibility and editor workflow.
